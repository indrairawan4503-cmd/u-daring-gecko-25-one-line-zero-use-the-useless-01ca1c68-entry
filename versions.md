# Variant C — version history (one concept, iterated v1 → v8)

Concept: a power over a household device that is precise but useless —
stopping the microwave at the exact millisecond the popcorn pops, but only
while the owner is in the shower. The twist: the precision itself is the
joke; the power always turns against its owner (dripping sprint, burnt
popcorn, screaming alarm, dinner eaten anyway).

## v1 — 150 chars
I can stop the microwave at the exact millisecond my popcorn finishes, but only when I'm in the shower, so I sprint back to a bag that's burnt anyway.
_Note: core joke lands, but short of the byte budget and the ending is flat._

## v2 — 165 chars
